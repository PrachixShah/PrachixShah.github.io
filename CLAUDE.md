## Keep as is (Prachi chose these; she reverted changes to them)
- Nav labels and look: `Home · About · Lab & Projects · Résumé · The Log · Bucket List ·
  Photos · Ethos`, uppercase mono. Do NOT switch to lowercase `/ slash` labels.

- Page titles, the soft-sage palette, `assets/style.css`. Ethos has its own design.

## Voice
- **Professional copy** (headline, résumé, project write-ups, About: On Circuits,
  On Asking Why) = keogan.ca style: plain, short, factual, understated. No superlatives,
  no bold lead-ins on résumé bullets.
- **Personal copy** (Some facts about me, About: On Cities, On People, Ethos) =
  ansonyu.me warmth: first person, parenthetical asides, the odd exclamation mark,
  "On ___" headings. Confident, never self-deprecating.
- Avoid AI tells: lists of three as a rhythm, "it isn't X, it's Y", dramatic one-line
  closers, calling the writing "honest", clever/punny headings, taglines and pull-quote
  blockquotes, em dashes everywhere.

## Positioning
- Headline: **"Circuits for physical things."** Analog and RF for circuits that sit next
  to a physical device: resonators, sensors, spin systems.
- The nanotech asset is knowing where a device's equivalent circuit comes from. Do NOT
  claim physics makes her better at general amplifier design.
- Target roles: acoustic RF filters (BAW/SAW), device modeling/characterization,
  MEMS/sensor front ends, quantum hardware control/readout.
