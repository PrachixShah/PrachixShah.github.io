About

Circuits for physical things. The first two sections are the work. The last two are everything else.

On Circuits

Analog and RF, for circuits that sit right next to a physical device: resonators, sensors, spin systems.

The circuit only sees the device through an equivalent circuit. I know where that model comes from. When I put water on my QCM crystal, the oscillator died. In the Butterworth–Van Dyke model, liquid loading shows up as a jump in motional resistance, which pushed my loop gain below one. That told me what to change.

My capstone is the same boundary from the other side. A maser’s 1.45 GHz output comes from a spin transition in the crystal, and my job is the optical pump that sets it up.
